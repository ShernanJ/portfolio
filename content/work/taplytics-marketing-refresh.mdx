<CaseSection id="lesson" label="Lesson" title="Good frontend work gives other teams leverage">
The useful part of this internship was realizing that frontend work does not end at matching a mockup. The better outcome was giving marketing a system they could keep using after the first page shipped.

It also taught me how much detail sits between design and implementation: CMS fields, empty states, image treatment, long copy, responsive behaviour, and reusable variants.
</CaseSection>
